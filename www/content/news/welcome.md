Today we're opening the doors to **Pasdiu** — the logistics hub where agencies, post-production studios, and freelance editor collectives run client work.

## Why Pasdiu

Media work has a shape: a client sends a brief, the work splits into projects and tasks, versions go back and forth, and eventually someone says *approved*. Most tools handle one slice of that. Pasdiu handles the whole pipeline:

- **Boards** that flip between Kanban and List, per project
- **The Iteration Room** — split-screen media with a v1/v2/v3 timeline and threaded feedback
- **A Client Portal** where reviewers approve for free, in unlimited numbers
- **The Export Ledger** — completed work, one click from an invoice-ready CSV

## Start free

The free plan is a complete product for a freelancer: 2 seats, 3 clients, 500 active tasks, and unlimited client reviewers. When you hire your third teammate or land your fourth client, Studio is $12 per seat per month.
